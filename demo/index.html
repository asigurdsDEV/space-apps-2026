About this recording and transcript

Rendered from the game at 1080p and 60 fps. The crew introduction is a staged local rehearsal. Spacecraft selection, the anomaly response, and the tested fix use the actual simulator. Live Earth imagery, weather and orbit source dates remain visible.

  • Students form a mission crew and design an Earth-observing satellite.
  • A larger spacecraft raises bus capacity from 4 to 9 kg and design cost from $5.05M to $6.59M.
  • The crew launches, deploys, and chooses how to respond to a ground-station interruption.
  • The failed mission scores 369. A same-mission simulator rerun with a faster radio succeeds at 967.
  • Live Earth shows the ISS, orbital debris, a typhoon and space weather using NASA, NOAA and CelesTrak sources.
  • First Flight is free and open source, built for students in under-resourced STEM classrooms.
